Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • Version 2.0.0
Executive Summary: British Radio Player is built with strict privacy standards. We collect zero personal data, zero advertising IDs, zero device identifiers, and zero location data. The app is completely ad-free, requires no account registration, and does not track you across other applications.

1. Who We Are

British Radio Player is an independent, open-source mobile application developed by Shai Vure (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This privacy policy governs the iOS application “British Radio Player” and related services hosted at https://shai.website/british-radio-player.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via email at shaivure@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many media streaming applications, British Radio Player does not collect personal identifiers. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Personal details: Your name, email address, postal address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Device identifiers: Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA), Vendor ID (IDFV), IMEI, or MAC address.
  • Account information: No username, password, or login credentials are required to use the app.
  • Location data: We never request or access your device's GPS or precise location.
  • Financial or payment data: The app is 100% free with no in-app purchases or subscription processing.
  • Tracking across apps: We do not link any data to third-party profiles or broker data to data brokers.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core application functionality, certain information is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (using Apple UserDefaults and local device caches):

  • Favorites: Your pinned live radio stations and subscribed podcasts.
  • Playback state: Your current listening progress in podcast episodes and playback speed preferences.
  • Downloaded episodes: Audio files downloaded for offline listening remain in the app's sandboxed local container.
  • App Preferences: Theme settings (Dark, Light, System), startup screen selection, and alarm configurations.
  • Last.fm credentials (Optional): If you choose to connect Last.fm, your credentials are stored locally in secure on-device storage solely to send scrobble requests to Last.fm's API on your behalf.

This local data never leaves your device unless you explicitly interact with an external service (such as Last.fm), and you can delete all local data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

4. Apple App Store Privacy Manifest Compliance

In full compliance with Apple's App Store Privacy Guidelines and iOS Privacy Manifest requirements (NSPrivacyTracking: false):

  • Tracking: British Radio Player does NOT track users across third-party apps or websites.
  • Required Reason APIs: The app utilizes standard Apple system APIs (such as file timestamp, disk space, and user defaults) strictly for app performance, cache eviction, and on-device settings storage, as declared in the app bundle manifest.

5. Optional Anonymous Telemetry

The app contains an optional telemetry system that is disabled by default. If you explicitly opt in within the app settings:

  • The app may send anonymous play event pings (e.g., station name or podcast ID, and a UTC timestamp).
  • No IP addresses are retained in database records.
  • No device fingerprints, UUIDs, or advertising IDs are attached to telemetry events.
  • This anonymous data is used solely to assess which streams are operational and popular, ensuring the reliability of public feeds.
  • You may opt out at any time in the app settings, which immediately ceases all telemetry transmissions.

6. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

When you play audio in British Radio Player, network requests are made directly to the broadcast servers delivering the content:

  • BBC Audio & Podcast Feeds: Audio streams and podcast RSS feeds originate from public BBC content delivery networks (including bbci.co.uk and Akamai). These servers may log standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and User-Agent) according to the BBC's own privacy policy and terms of service.
  • Last.fm (Optional): If you voluntarily connect your Last.fm account, track titles and artist names are submitted to Last.fm in accordance with Last.fm's Privacy Policy.
  • No Third-Party Ad Networks: We do not integrate any advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network).
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate tracking SDKs such as Google Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or Facebook SDK.

7. Children's Privacy

British Radio Player does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for general audiences.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date”. We recommend reviewing this policy occasionally.
